# CyberScore Pro — Methodology

## Origin

Developed by Vijay L Narasimhan, 2025–26, to make cybersecurity
self-assessment accessible to businesses (particularly SMEs) that lack
in-house security expertise but need a credible, structured way to
understand their risk posture before engaging specialist support.

## Problem this solves

Formal cybersecurity frameworks (e.g. NIST CSF, Cyber Essentials, ISO 27001)
are thorough but often too dense or resource-intensive for a business owner
to self-assess against quickly. CyberScore Pro translates the underlying
principles of these frameworks into a fast, plain-language self-assessment
that still produces a credible, actionable score.

## Inputs → Process → Outputs

- **Inputs:** answers to the structured cybersecurity questionnaire
- **Process:** domain-weighted scoring against recognised control categories
- **Outputs:** overall CyberScore, per-domain breakdown, prioritised
  plain-language recommendations

## Why this approach (rationale)

Existing frameworks are authoritative but not designed for rapid business
self-assessment. CyberScore Pro's contribution is the translation layer —
making the recognised control domains usable in minutes rather than requiring
a formal audit — while keeping the scoring traceable back to established
practice, so the output remains credible to a security-literate reviewer.
